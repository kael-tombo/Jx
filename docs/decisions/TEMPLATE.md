# ADR template

An ADR records a decision, not a diary entry. One per file, named
`NNNN-brief-slug.md`, where NNNN is a zero-padded sequence number. Use MADR
(short). Content borrowed from https://github.com/adr/madr/blob/main/template.md.

```markdown
# NNNN. <Title>

Date: YYYY-MM-DD
Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN

## Context and problem

What forces are in play, including the brief's own decisions that motivate this.

## Decision

What we decided, in one sentence, then the details.

## Options considered

* Option A — why not.
* Option B — why not or why chosen.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, harder, or is deliberately deferred. Any VERIFY items.
```
