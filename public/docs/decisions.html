How to read a record

Each record states the forces in play, the decision, the options considered and the consequences. A record is dated, so it can be read in the order it was written.

The template lives beside the records in the repository. A new decision starts as a proposal and becomes accepted only after review.

Why the site has records

The website is a design artifact too. Its type scale, truth rules and fonts are recorded so a later change can argue with a decision instead of guessing at one. The three site records use numbers from 0100 so they stay clear of the tool's own ADR sequence.
